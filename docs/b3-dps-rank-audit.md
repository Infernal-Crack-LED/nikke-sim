## 3. Already-known kit findings on the MAJOR-flagged units

Straight from `data/kit-status.json` — where a flag already has a recorded explanation, it is not a new discovery.

**Raven (`raven`)**

- med ENGINE (skill1 (cadence input, engine/charFixes — not the override block)): The DoT-instance count rests on a 22-frame bolt-recovery gap being applied to an RL, which the mechanics SSOT says RL does NOT have — an unverified ~27% shot-rate (and therefore DoT-output) swing on her dominant damage bucket.
- med ENGINE (base weapon data (data/characters.json → raven) — OUT OF KIT-LINE SCOPE, reported not acted on): HYPOTHESIS: raven's `chargeMultiplier` is 0 while her datamined `full_charge_damage` is 25000 (=250%), so her full-charge rocket may be resolving with a Charge bucket of 1.0 (or 0) instead of 2.5 — a potential 2.5× under-credit on her normal-attack channel that no agent in this gauntlet mentions.

**Asuka: WILLE (`asuka-wille`)**

- med FIDELITY (test-header (scripts/tests/units/asuka-wille.test.ts)): The driver's unit-test docblock is STALE and now contradicts both the shipped override and the test's own assertions — it documents the pre-gauntlet encoding as if it were current.
- low ENCODING (skill2 (Emergency Repair Effect 4)): 'Reload speed is fixed at a 60% increase for 1 rounds' is modeled as a 10.5-second window, which at MG cadence can accelerate roughly two reloads where the kit grants one.
- low FIDELITY (burst / skill1 (Annihilation State proxy)): A SELF mode is represented as a fabricated status inflicted on the boss, which is an unguarded side channel any other unit reading target statuses could pick up.
- low SILENT_DROP (burst (Annihilation finisher precondition)): 'Affects the target(s) afflicted with Anti A.T. Field' — the finisher's affliction precondition is nowhere in the override: no gate, no caveat, no `unmodeled` entry.
- low ENGINE (skill2 vs burst (two proxies for one in-game instant)): Annihilation-State end is modeled twice with different clocks — burstCast+delaySec 9 for the finisher, fullBurstEnd for Emergency Repair — so two effects the kit fires simultaneously land ~1s apart.
