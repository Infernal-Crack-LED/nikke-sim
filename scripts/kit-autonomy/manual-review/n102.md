## Kit summary

N102 is a Water rocket-launcher Supporter on Burst I (cd 20s) with **no personal-damage lines at all**
— no riders, no DoTs, no nukes. Her entire contribution is three buffs she places on others:

- **Skill 1 "Written Memories"** — every time she fires a Full Charge attack (and an RL fires *only*
  full-charge shots), she grants the **single ally with the highest final ATK** +3 Max Ammunition
  Capacity and +10.34% Critical Damage, both for 10s. At her rocket charge cadence (~1.5–2s/charge)
  the 10s window refreshes continuously, so both grants sit at ~100% uptime on the carry after the
  first charge.
- **Skill 2 "Impaired Memory"** — on a 15s cooldown she grants that same highest-final-ATK ally
  +25.84% Charge Damage for 5s (≈33% uptime — a pulse, never always-on).
- **Burst "Memories of Blue Butterflies"** — when she casts, **all allies** (herself included) gain
  +25.86% ATK for 10s.

Because every line is a buff, the whole assertable surface is the `buffApply` events she emits — their
stat, value, duration, target set, and cadence. The discriminations that matter: the S1/S2 grants are
scoped to exactly one ally (the highest-final-ATK carry, not self, not the whole team); the S1 ammo
grant is a FLAT round count (`maxAmmoFlat`, not a percent); the S2 grant is additive charge-bucket
points (`chargeDamagePct`, not the multiplicative `chargeDamageMultPct`) on an interval CD (not
passive, not burst-keyed); and the burst ATK grant scales each recipient's OWN ATK (`atkPct`, not a
caster-scaled flat add) and reaches the whole team.

## Residual flags for owner

1. **⚑ S2 interval cadence + first-fire phase (MEASUREMENT-GATED, global convention).** The 15s period
   comes from the datamined `skillCooldownsSec.skill2 = 15` (the blind role lacked it and flagged the
   same gap), and the first fire at t=15 follows the repo's standing `interval` first-fire convention
   (⚑ phase — t=sec vs t=0). A popup-cadence read on an N102-focused ally settles the real period and
   phase. This is an inherited global convention, not an n102-specific claim.
2. **⚑ `byFinalAtk` live-vs-static ranking (unobservable in fixture).** The kit says "highest FINAL ATK"
   → live `effectiveAtk` ranking (the A3 literal-word rule). In every fixture tried, static and live
   ranking resolve to the same Attacker-class carry, so the flag is cosmetic here; it would only matter
   in a comp where another buffer's single-target ATK grant flips which ally is highest mid-fight.
3. **⚑ full-charge trigger read.** "Activates when performing a Full Charge attack" is read as
   `shotFired` because an RL fires only full-charge shots (the helm precedent for the same phrasing on a
   charge weapon). Observationally exact today; if the engine ever emits an uncharged RL shot, the S1
   grants would over-fire (bounded — the 10s window is refresh-saturated either way).
4. **Cadence tuple (datamine).** `chargeFrames 90` / `reloadFrames 141` / ammo 6 are datamined and drive
   only N102's OWN shot cadence (hence her S1 refresh rate and burst gauge); her SUPPORT buff magnitudes
   are kit-exact and not cadence-dependent.
5. **Tier stays MODEL_ONLY.** The gauntlet certifies STRUCTURE (faithfulness), not magnitudes — N102 keeps
   `tier:MODEL_ONLY`, `tuned:false` until a real fight validates her numbers. Board row is null (no
   recordings yet).
