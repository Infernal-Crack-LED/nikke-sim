## Kit summary

Jackal is an Iron rocket-launcher Defender on Burst I (cd 20s) — a tank whose kit is almost
entirely a damage-mitigation package, and whose two offensive levers are exactly the two lines
the sim's engine cannot express. When attacked 10 times she marks the highest-Max-HP enemy with
Damage Taken ▲9.09% and ATK ▼9.09% for 10s; at battle start she links herself and the two
highest-final-ATK allies to share damage taken and raises their DEF ▲8.27% for 120s; her Burst I
raises all allies' DEF ▲14.69% for 10s and — her headline — boosts by 38.91% for 15s the Burst
Skill damage of skills whose description says "Affects 1 enemy unit(s)". On the scope-lock basis
(immortal boss that never acts) the attacked-counter can never fire, there is no damage pool to
share, there is no Burst-Skill-Damage bucket in the formula SSOT, and DEF feeds no damage path —
so her modeled kit is two inert defPct grants and her sim output is her bare RL weapon. Her
comps read COLD by exactly the two documented gap amounts; that is honest omission, not fudge.
