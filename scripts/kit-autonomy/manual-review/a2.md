## Kit summary

A2 is a Fire RL Attacker whose burst ("Mode B") deals NO damage — it is a pure self state. Mode B raises
her ATK (+15.19%) and Charge Speed (+35.88%) while it drains her own HP (3.99% of CURRENT HP every second),
and it removes itself once her HP dips below 40%. Because the sim models no HP pool, the removal is captured
as a DERIVED uptime: the compound drain HP(t)=0.9601^t crosses the stated 40% floor at
t = ln(0.4)/ln(0.9601) = 22.5s from full HP, so Mode B is a finite ~22.5s window per 40s burst cycle
(~56% uptime with a ~17.5s dead gap), NOT a permanent buff. Charge Speed is subtractive on her 60-frame
charge time, so it gates how many full-charge rockets she fires — it is damage, not utility. Her Skill 1
("Output Increased") is a burstCast self window adding +110.44% Charge Damage for 15s — the ADDITIVE
flat-points stat (`chargeDamagePct`), lifting an RL full-charge shot's charge multiplier from the bare 2.5
to 2.5 + 1.1044 = 3.6044 (NOT the base-scaling `chargeDamageMultPct`, which would give 2.5 × 2.1044 = 5.261).
Her Skill 2 ("Slow Charge Attack") fires on every full-charge hit (every RL pull): a 30.1%-of-final-ATK
additional-damage rider at the target plus a 3s self Damage-to-Parts +40.88% buff (parsed but inert on the
partless boss). In the control fixture (liter/crown/a2/helm, boss Fire, focus a2) she deals ~265M; removing
the Mode B state, the S1 charge-damage window, or the rider each drop her total (all three are live).

## Residual flags (owner spot-check cluster)

1. **Mode B derived duration 22.5s (med, documented, not blocking):** the kit gives NO "for N sec" — Mode B
   lasts until HP < 40% under a 3.99%/s drain, which the sim cannot model (no HP pool). The driver enacts the
   compound (CURRENT-HP) closed-form ln(0.4)/ln(0.9601) = 22.5s; the linear (MAX-HP) reading would be
   60/3.99 = 15.04s. Both blind writers independently derived the same 22.5s ceiling. Resolve with one A2
   recording: frame-count from the burst banner to the frame her Charge-Speed/ATK buff icons clear (or her HP
   bar hits 40%). If Mode B survives past ~22s, the drain is not on current HP (or she is being healed) — re-derive.
2. **Full-HP-at-cast assumption (low):** the 22.5s assumes A2 is at full HP at each burst cast. Real fights with
   boss damage shorten Mode B; healing that offsets the drain extends it. Healing-based extension (crown heals in
   the fixture) is out of scope with no HP pool and is NOT modeled.
3. **shotFired full-charge proxy (low):** "when hitting a target with Full Charge" has no schema trigger kind;
   encoded `shotFired` because every RL pull IS a full charge (chargeFrames 60), so pulls and full-charge hits
   are 1:1. It is an on-fire proxy for an on-hit event (lands ~0 frames early; no delaySec modeled). Popup-count
   one magazine (ammo 6): exactly 6 extra ~30.1%-of-ATK popups confirms the 1:1 mapping.
4. **rider gauge-gen cast-frame timing (low, emergent):** the rider generates burst gauge (skillGauge, SSOT §6),
   so it shifts A2's cast-frame alignment and ripple-affects teammate totals ~0.1–0.7% — a faithful engine
   behaviour, flagged so a future reader doesn't mistake the coupling for a bug.
5. **Explosion Radius (out of v1 scope):** no radius StatKey exists; against the single partless boss a larger
   radius changes nothing. Becomes actionable only on a multi-target or parted boss.
