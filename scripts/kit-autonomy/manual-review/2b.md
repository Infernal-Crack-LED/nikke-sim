## Kit summary

2B is a Fire AR Defender whose entire offense scales with how often she personally casts her burst.
Her Skill 1 ("Survival") permanently raises her OWN Max HP in escalating cumulative steps keyed to
her own burst casts — +10.03% on the first, +20.06% more on the second, +57.76% more on the third,
capping at +87.85% total ("each subsequent effect triggers all effects before it" = the Liter-validated
cumulative ladder). Her Skill 2 passive ("Cluster Bomb" passive half) converts 6.16% of her CURRENT
(final, live) Max HP into ATK — so every Max HP stack makes every hit she deals harder; this conversion
is her real damage engine and is what makes the Skill 1 HP grants OFFENSIVE rather than defensive
(self-granted Max HP feeds the conversion because caster === target, satisfying the engine's e3 rule).
The other half of Skill 2 fires a 167.45%-of-final-ATK cluster bomb at the enemy every 300 rounds she
fires (recurring `hitCount`). Her burst ("Series of Attacks") fires two instant hits at cast time — a
2439.36%-of-final-ATK DISTRIBUTED hit against all enemies plus a separate 792%-of-final-ATK plain hit
against the highest-HP enemy — both landing BEFORE the Full Burst window opens (instant cast), so
neither takes the +50% FB major. In the control fixture she deals ~323M (≈27% of team) with the
conversion live; removing the conversion crashes her to ~94M (the meta-defining line).

## Residual flags (owner spot-check cluster)

1. **crit encoding on the 3 damage lines (med, documented, not blocking):** driver non-crit default vs blind
   crit:true rider-prior. The formula SSOT's damage-type table has skill "% of final ATK" crit ON and burst
   nuke crit ON, and the popup-verified Cinderella worked example shows a burst nuke critting — so the SSOT
   leans toward the blind. Resolve with a focus-2B recording: read popup colours/values on the 167.45% rider
   and the 2439.36/792 burst hits (a crit popup is exactly ×1.5 its non-crit sibling at base crit damage). If
   crits are observed, set `crit:true` on all three flatDamage lines (~+7.5% expected value); if never observed
   across a full fight, keep the default and close the flag.
2. **cumulative 87.85% escalation reading (same-family residual):** corroborated by the Liter-validated
   escalating precedent, but the "each subsequent effect triggers all effects before it" → additive 87.85%
   cap originates from claude-family blind agents (S2b/S5/S6 all converged on it). A latest-line-only game
   (57.76% cap) would shrink her ATK conversion ~30%. Board A/B or a focus Max-HP read adjudicates.
3. **recurring-every-300 vs one-time cluster bomb (same-family residual):** encoded recurring (engine hitCount
   convention, ~6 procs/fight); a one-time 300-shot trigger would give exactly 1 proc. A focus-video popup
   count is the discriminator.
4. **same-frame ordering convention (⚑):** the engine applies the Nth burst's S1 Max HP tier BEFORE that
   cast's 2439.36/792 hits snapshot ATK, so cast N's nuke already benefits from tier N (observed cast amounts
   14.7M→17.1M→22.0M, plateau at the cap). Whether the game applies the tier before or after the same cast's
   damage is unmeasured; the spec pins only the ordering-robust GROWTH, not the exact tier index.
5. **Preparer slug edge case (tooling, surfaced for a durable fix):** `prepare-cross-family-packet.ts`'s
   display-name needle "2b" substring-collides with the "S2b" role label in the s2b template title (a pure
   false positive — the role label is in every unit's packet and leaks nothing). Worked around with a temporary
   template-title edit (restored byte-identical). Recommended durable fix: give the display-name needle
   word-boundary treatment, consistent with the 2026-07-25 short-slug fix, so numeric-named units (2B) don't
   false-positive on the S2b/S5/S6 role labels.
