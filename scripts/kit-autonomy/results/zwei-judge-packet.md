## 2. Damage-formula + PIERCE SSOT (the facts the verdict turns on)

Damage = ATK × major (×1.10 element if advantaged) × charge × damageUp-bucket × taken × distributed.
**Pierce routing (engine sim.ts:1400-1401):** `pierceDamagePct` ("Pierce Damage ▲") feeds the Damage-Up
bucket ONLY on a Pierce-TAGGED hit. A hit is pierce-tagged iff the holder has static `hasPierce`, a live timed
`gainPierce` window (`pierceUntilFrame`), or a per-shot tag (`weaponSwap.hasPierce` on a swap shot). So a
`pierceDamagePct` buff on an ally that is NOT pierce-tagged moves ZERO damage for that ally.
`gainPierce` effect = "Gain Pierce: the target's attacks count as Pierce-tagged, so its (and teammates')
Pierce Damage ▲ buffs go live" (a pierce GRANT). `weaponSwap.hasPierce` = "Additional Effect: Pierce" scoped
to the swapped weapon only.
**Round-count duration:** "for N round(s)" = `durationShots:N` (holder-scoped, expires after the holder fires
N rounds, NO wall-clock expiry), NEVER `durationSec`.
**critRatePct vs critRateNormalPct:** plain "Critical Rate ▲" = generic `critRatePct` (lifts crit on EVERY
bucket); "Critical Rate of normal attacks ▲" = scoped `critRateNormalPct`.
**Gates available:** fbGate(inFb/outFb), swapGate(swapped/unswapped), requiresTargetStatus (ENEMY status only,
name-keyed), requiresCore, everyN, resourceGate, formation/teamHas. There is NO "while THIS ally holds buff X"
gate primitive — an ally-held status gate must be PROXIED (fbGate or swapGate).
