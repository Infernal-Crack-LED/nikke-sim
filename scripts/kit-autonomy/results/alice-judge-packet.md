## 2. Damage-formula SSOT (summary) + the two charge stats
Damage = ATK × major (×1.10 element if advantaged) × charge × damageUp-bucket × taken × distributed.
TWO distinct charge stats in the engine (this is load-bearing for alice):
- `chargeDamagePct` = "additive percentage points in the charge bucket" (types.ts:23; sim.ts:1385 adds stat/100 flat). "Charge Damage ▲7%" → this.
- `chargeDamageMultPct` = "scales by BASE charge damage" (types.ts:24; sim.ts:1383 baseCharge×pct — collection items / Helm max-treasure burst). NOT an ordinary "Charge Damage ▲" buff.
- `chargeSpeedPct` shortens charge time: needed = round(chargeFrames × (1 − cs/100)) (sim.ts:2557-2560) → load-bearing for a charge SR (more charged shots). `attackSpeedPct` instead scales fire rate (sim.ts:2600/2616), NOT charge time.
- Pierce: `hasPierce` is a hit TAG enabling `pierceDamagePct` eligibility (sim.ts:1400); PIERCE_CORE_DOUBLE=false (sim.ts:1091) so NO core+body double-hit. With no pierceDamagePct source and a partless boss, hasPierce is damage-INERT at scope lock.
- Scope lock: boss deals NO damage and there is NO HP pool, so any HP-gated / lifesteal line is unreachable and moves no damage.
