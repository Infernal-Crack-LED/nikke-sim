- casterAtkPct = a FLAT add of % of the CASTER's ATK (buffApply.value is the RESOLVED flat grant, not the kit percentage). atkPct scales the target's OWN ATK.
- casterMaxHpPct = a FLAT add of % of the CASTER's Max HP; resolves to a maxHpFlat buffApply. Ally-granted Max HP never feeds a teammate's atkOfMaxHpPct conversion (offensively inert here).
- projectileExplosionPct feeds ONLY projectile-explosion-flavored damage (RL explosion + Anis's Shooting Stars dot carry that flavor); inert on non-RL/non-explosion kits.
